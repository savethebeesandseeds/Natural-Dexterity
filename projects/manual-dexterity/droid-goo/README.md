# Droid Goo

Droid Goo (also tentatively called **Droid Slime**) is a dream of a soft,
squishy robotic toy, a sibling to [Droid Blocks](../droid-blocks/README.md).
Imagine a little blob that could wiggle, change shape, respond to touch, and
explore its surroundings with a playful personality.

The hope is something you could interact with and play alongside: stretch it,
reshape it, and see how it responds. A small piece of goo that feels alive with
curiosity could be an incredible toy.

For now, this is just an exploratory concept, with no prototype or settled
technical approach. This folder is a place to collect ideas and possibilities,
and perhaps one day begin turning the dream into something real.
