\begin{abstract}
This study implements minimum-entropy $\mathcal H_\infty$ control of a
simulated helicopter. Its objective describes closed-loop disturbance response,
not thermodynamic entropy. Numerical synthesis checks and seven paired
nonlinear flight cases pass, with explicit PID landing support.
\end{abstract}
\begin{IEEEkeywords}
Helicopter, minimum entropy, robust control.
\end{IEEEkeywords}
\balance
\section{Define the Control Objective}
For a stable, strictly proper closed-loop map $T:w\mapsto z$, disturbance
and noise inputs $w$ and regulated outputs $z$ have declared weights.
The Mustafa--Glover problem requires internal stability,
$\|T\|_\infty<\gamma$, and minimizes~\cite{mustafa1990,lessard2014}
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal I_\gamma(T)&=-\frac{\gamma^2}{2\pi}
 \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\ln\det W(\omega)\,d\omega,\\
W(\omega)&=I-\gamma^{-2}T(j\omega)^*T(j\omega).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The star denotes conjugate transpose. This frequency-domain criterion approaches
$\|T\|_2^2$ as $\gamma\to\infty$. It is not heat generation in J/K and does not
promise lower electrical consumption. Robustness to model uncertainty requires
a specified uncertainty interconnection and bound.

\section{Synthesize the Central Controller}
A nominal hover linearization and weighting filters define
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\dot x_\ell&=Ax_\ell+B_1w+B_2u,\\
z&=C_1x_\ell+D_{12}u,\\
y&=C_2x_\ell+D_{21}w .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The design uses 20 local mechanical states, with three attitude-error coordinates;
thermal states remain in the nonlinear plant. Three stable position filters, $\dot\eta=e_p-0.04\eta$, extend the design to 23 states. Twelve kinematic sensors, six acceleration disturbances and twelve noise inputs define the normalized channels. All weights and matrices are published.

The normalized DGKF construction requires stabilizability, detectability, channel
normalization, stabilizing nonnegative Riccati solutions $X,Y$, and
$\rho(XY)<\gamma^2$~\cite{dgkf1989}. Setting the free controller parameter to zero
gives the minimum-entropy realization at the chosen feasible $\gamma$.

The online law updates a dynamic state,
$\dot x_K=A_Kx_K+B_Ky$, $u_\delta=C_Kx_K$;
the Riccati problems are solved before flight. A zero-order-hold realization updates at 100 Hz; sampled stability is checked separately.

\section{Declare the Nonlinear Boundary}
The 2\,kg uncalibrated plant retains nonlinear rotor, thermal and contact
dynamics. MuJoCo advances the rigid body at 0.01\,s; rotor states use RK4
with governed rotor speeds.
Both controllers receive matching sensor noise and no true wind or mass in
feedback. The simulator supplies a spooled, mass-specific initial trim;
discovering it is not demonstrated. Pitch/slew limits, reference feedforward,
anti-windup and PID landing support lie outside the linear theorem.
Every hold and support step counts in the 70\,s mission.
\section{Validation and Project}
The nominal design has $\gamma=1.5134$ and a numerical bounded-real gain
upper bound of $1.3389$. The closed loop is stable; its sampled spectral radius
is $0.99960008$. The entropy is $17.5046$ and the H2 norm is $3.6367$;
independent Riccati checks, Lyapunov calculation, refined frequency integration,
and a large-$\gamma$ limit check agree. 

All 15 trials pass: seven 70\,s cases for each controller, plus a disabled-control
check. Cases cover wind/gusts, $\pm20\%$ mass and threefold sensor noise.
No audited physical or landing limits are violated. $\mathcal H_\infty$ RMS error is
$0.082$--$0.143$\,m, versus $0.139$--$0.239$\,m for the retuned PID.
$\mathcal H_\infty$ supplies $93.04$--$93.16\%$ of all commands; the rest are trim hold and
PID landing support. No failure fallback occurs.

Limiters do intervene, especially under increased sensor noise. The linear
certificate therefore does not prove the complete nonlinear flights robust.
These paired results establish neither universal superiority nor hardware
performance. Energy and thermal entropy are separate measurements.\par
