Peek inside the experiment +

This first construction kit has two beams, one powered base hinge and a freely moving elbow. Blocks change the body’s mass and balance. The attached eye measures its own acceleration and turning.

The engine uses native 3D physics with joints that keep this creature’s motion in a plane. This anchored kit has no collisions: parts can pass through each other. The page displays engine positions; it does not invent motion. Exploration tries movement patterns. It is not reward optimization or a trained policy.

Sensor axes move with the eye. Felt acceleration is accelerometer specific force, including the effect of support under gravity; it is not acceleration in world coordinates.
