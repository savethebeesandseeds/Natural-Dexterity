Peek inside this experiment +

The body has two beams, one powered hinge and a passive elbow. Native 3D physics moves the body in a plane; parts can pass through each other. The light sensor is a physical mounted part: both its position and the direction of its receiving face affect what it sees.

Controller details will appear when the engine connects.

The light sensor delivers sampled readings with a delay. Moving the source does not erase already sampled light or reset the body.

“Leave the motor quiet”, “Follow a rhythm”, and “Try random moves” are comparisons without learning. The chart reports what was measured in this try. It does not establish a general learning result.
