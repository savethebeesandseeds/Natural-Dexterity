What is learning here? +

The saved actor and critic belong to a PPO controller. “Keep learning” starts with those weights and updates them after new interactions. “Learn from scratch” starts with fresh weights and gets the same interaction budget. The frozen policy still reacts to its current senses.

The policy reads a short stack of local motor feedback, delayed IMU and light measurements, and its recent actions and rewards. The motor’s hinge index, body geometry and sun position stay outside that input.
