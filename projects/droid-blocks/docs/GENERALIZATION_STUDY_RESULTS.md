# Frozen first-use behavior on new two-link geometries

**Collection completed: 2026-10-06.** Batch:
`artifacts/reports/generalization-study-v1-20261006`.
**Independent native replay audit: PASS.** All 322 retained tapes passed
23,876,934 checks with zero failures; the declared gates were independently
reproduced.

## Outcome and bounded meaning

The prospectively declared comparison passed its source-release, reserved
breadth, useful-behavior and fixed-witness criteria. After equal offline
experience, pooled pretraining exceeded narrow pretraining's mean reserved
return in all three training seeds. All 36 pooled frozen runs on twelve new
length-5 configurations safely completed, exceeded quiet by more than +3.2,
and beat their matched saved initial neural policy.

This is evidence of useful frozen behavior on new geometries within one
anchored, collisionless planar two-link graph. It supports a bounded part of
the owner's requirement that the first assembly inherit competence from
simulation training. It does not establish arbitrary creature topology,
variable motor/sensor counts, contact, walking, hardware transfer or an
acceptable child's waiting time. Each policy still received 64 simulated
seconds of causal sensor/history feedback from wake-up, with no gradient
updates, free warmup, rescue reset or history from another case.

The benefit of breadth is uneven. Pooled beat narrow in **21 of 36** paired
case/seed comparisons. Their overall mean difference was **+2.604376**, while
the case-pair median was only **+0.505825**, with a range **-21.257950 to
+41.710906**. Narrow also descriptively cleared the quiet margin in all 36
reserved runs. Broad training improved the declared average comparison; it
was not necessary for useful motion on every member of this limited family.

The primary median of three seed means is **+1.723678**, with **3/3 positive**.
Seed 101's case-pair median was negative despite its positive mean; large
individual gains do not establish uniform superiority. The three training
seeds are the policy replicates. Cases within a seed share one trained policy
and are correlated; a shared quiet return is one deterministic baseline per

## What remains to establish

The released twelve length-5 conditions are now known exposure. Any descendant
that uses these outcomes, witnesses, policies or design conclusions must obtain
a fresh, prospectively versioned holdout for a new generalization claim. Do not
rerun these bodies and call them unseen, select seed 303 for its larger mean,
or relax the recorded source-release rule.

The next product benchmark needs an explicit supported assembly domain,
first-response latency and modest specialization allowance. Bounded adaptation
was not executed here. Changed connectivity, variable sensor placement,
multiple motors, preferences, contact/support/recovery and hardware require
their own mechanisms and evidence. This result advances fixed-graph geometry
generalization; it leaves the broader first-creature requirement open.
