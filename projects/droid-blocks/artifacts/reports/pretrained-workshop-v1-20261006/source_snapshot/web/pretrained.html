Peek inside its saved brain +

This controller was pretrained across 32 simulation conditions. Rebuilding changes the body and clears its recent sensor history; it does not train or change the saved policy. Its choices use local motor, motion and light feedback, without receiving the body layout or the lamp position.

Choices remain stochastic: the portable sampler matches the policy's probabilities, rather than Torch's seeded action sequence.

This demo uses the first declared training seed, 101. Its supported choices are three body layouts, two motor placements and two lamp positions within the same two-link graph. The research result does not establish arbitrary creature topology, walking, hardware transfer or a child's waiting time.
