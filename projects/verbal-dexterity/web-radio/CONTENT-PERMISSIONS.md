Access or listening does not grant permission to redistribute or rehost copies,
rebroadcast or publicly perform the content, publish excerpts, make or share
adaptations, translations or remixes, or reuse protected character expression.
Commercial reuse also needs separate permission: for example, selling copies,
using content in advertising, offering a monetized rebroadcast, or incorporating
it into a commercial product or service. This applies to the rights Waajacu
controls, subject to the exceptions below.

Waajacu may grant separate written permissions, including paid commercial
licenses, for rights it actually holds. Request permission through the
[repository owner](https://github.com/savethebeesandseeds/Natural-Dexterity),
identifying the work or release and the proposed use. A grant from Waajacu does
not clear rights held by someone else. Credit alone is not permission.

## Third-party material, earlier grants, and legal exceptions

Third-party or user-provided music, performances, voice recordings, artwork,
text, and other assets retain their own rights and terms. Permission to use a
model or a prebuilt voice is not ownership of that model or voice.

This policy does not revoke earlier grants, rights under applicable platform
terms, or a separate license for an identified work. Existing grants continue
under their own terms. Where a release includes a separate permission, state
which material it covers; the rest remains subject to its applicable terms.

Nothing here limits fair use, fair dealing, or another use permitted by law
without Waajacu's permission. It does not claim rights in ideas, public-domain
material, independently created works, or other unprotected elements.

## AI-assisted and generated material

This notice creates no rights where applicable law provides none. Waajacu does
not assert exclusive copyright in wholly AI-generated elements that lack
protection. Any claim is limited to rights actually held, such as qualifying
human-authored expression, creative edits or arrangements, or recording rights
recognized by applicable law. Generated output may be similar to someone else's;
use of a generator or a copyright notice alone does not establish exclusivity.
